The useful bits

What happens if you book me?

Nothing mysterious. We talk, plan what actually matters and make sure you can enjoy the wedding instead of managing the photography.

first

The date, venue, ceremonies, guest count, anything that makes it yours. I'll let you know availability and send the relevant guide.

We get the important things out of your head and into mine.

Family groups, timings, traditions, people to look out for, the part you're most excited about. Planning means less directing on the day.
