Mint by Sajeda is owned and operated by American photographer Sajeda Panju. I've photographed weddings professionally since 2009, from intimate ceremonies to multi-day celebrations across the UK and abroad.

My style leans toward the candid, organic in-between moments, but I'm not going to leave you stranded when you need direction. We'll make the portraits, we'll get the family photographs, and then we'll get you back to your wedding.
